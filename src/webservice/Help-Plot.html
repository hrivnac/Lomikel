Plot

Plots created from graph data appear in this tab. Plot types include per-object LightCurves, scatter plots, and correlograms. Click one object vertex and choose LightCurve to plot its available ZTF g/r or LSST u/g/r/i/z/y detections in separate colors. The horizontal axis shows calendar dates (ZTF UTC from JD, LSST TAI from midpointMjdTai); the magnitude axis is inverted (brighter is higher). ZTF plots PSF magnitude; LSST converts science-image forced flux in nJy to AB science magnitude. Hover or focus on a point for its date, source ID, magnitude and uncertainty. LSST nonpositive science flux cannot become a magnitude and is counted as excluded rather than fabricated.

For other plots, select an element or open a table and use its actions.
