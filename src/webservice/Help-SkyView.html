SkyView

Select an object vertex in the graph and choose SkyView in Selection actions. This plots every object currently in the graph (including objects inside collapsed clusters), not just the selected one. Alternatively, open an object table and click Sky View to plot the objects in its rows. Both actions look up positions in Fink Elasticsearch and use the same map; ZTF objects are orange and LSST objects blue. For LSST Solar System objects, SkyView requests all source rows returned by Fink's SSO endpoint, checks their coverage of Elasticsearch's deduplicated positions and, where present, times, and connects the observation-level rows in MJD (TAI) order. Elasticsearch alone cannot prove the number of distinct observations, so this view depends on Fink's documented full-history SSO endpoint. Historical markers become smaller when points are close, leaving the blue trajectory visible. Small object IDs appear beside static positions and the latest SS source; hovering over any source shows its ID and time. The map and its zoom and download buttons fit the SkyView pane. Open Map settings for the other sky controls, or expand Plotted object coordinates for the RA/Dec and SS source list.

Objects without coordinates are counted but not plotted. SkyView refuses a history that fails its available-index coverage or size checks instead of presenting it as complete; it plots at most 12,000 positions and 20 SS objects. During the ss_mjd backfill, a trajectory whose times cannot be cross-checked against Elasticsearch is labeled in the map summary; its plotted time/position pairs come from Fink source rows and its indexed positions are still checked. The table's existing Sky coordinate route remains available for non-object rows containing ra and dec.
