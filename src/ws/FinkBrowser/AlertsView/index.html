Recent Fink alerts are also available as links in the Recent alerts panel.

Recent astronomical alerts on the night sky. Fink classifies detections from survey telescopes so unusual or rapidly changing objects can be followed up.

  • Follow alerts moves the view toward currently animated alerts.
  • Whole sky shows the complete celestial sphere.
  • Five-pointed markers are ZTF alerts; ten-pointed markers are LSST alerts.
  • Point to or tap a marker for details. Keyboard users can open the same objects from Recent alerts.
  • ZTF polling is active. LSST polling is paused while recent LSST alerts are unavailable.
  • Optional URL parameters:
    fetchPeriod=10&fetchStart=48&nAlerts=10&magMax=6&fetchLSST=false
